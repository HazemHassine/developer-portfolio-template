---
title: "Anatomy of a useful project case study"
date: "AUGUST 22, 2026"
summary: "A starter outline for writing project stories that go beyond a list of technologies."
---

A strong case study helps a reader understand your judgment, not only the final interface. Start with the user or system problem, then explain the constraints that shaped your work.

## Show the decisions

Describe one or two alternatives you considered. Explain why the selected approach fit the project at that moment, including any tradeoffs you accepted.

## Add evidence

Whenever possible, include a measurable result: latency, adoption, errors prevented, time saved, accessibility improvements, or a verified learning outcome.

## Close with reflection

State what you would keep, what you would change, and what you would investigate next. This makes the project feel like real engineering work instead of a polished artifact with no history.
