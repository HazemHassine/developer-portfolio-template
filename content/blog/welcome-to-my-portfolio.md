## Editing local posts

Every Markdown file in `content/blog` becomes a post. The filename is the URL slug, while the frontmatter controls the title, date, and summary.

Add normal Markdown below the frontmatter. The template calculates reading time automatically and lists the newest posts first.

## Make it yours

Useful first posts might explain a project decision, document something you learned, or walk through a problem that took longer to solve than expected.
